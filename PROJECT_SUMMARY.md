# Project Summary

## Problem Statement

Customer reviews contain valuable product feedback, but unstructured reviews can make it difficult to identify recurring issues and prioritize improvements.

## Approach

The project uses a small sample of Zomato user reviews and an AI-assisted analysis workflow to:

* Organize user feedback.
* Identify recurring themes.
* Group common customer pain points.
* Prioritize improvement opportunities.
* Translate feedback into actionable product recommendations.

## Analysis Areas

The collected reviews were analyzed across areas including:

* Customer Support
* Delivery
* Pricing
* Order Accuracy
* Cancellation and Refunds
* Food Quality
* Product Information
* Payment Options
* Product Expansion and Membership
* Usability and Restaurant Discovery

## Key Finding

Customer support, delivery, and pricing were among the most frequently occurring themes in the collected sample.

## Deliverables

* Anonymized review dataset
* Theme-level analysis
* Product recommendations
* Theme-frequency visualization
* Project documentation

## Limitations

The analysis is based on a limited sample of 15 collected reviews. The results are intended for portfolio and educational purposes and should not be treated as representative of the complete Zomato user population.
