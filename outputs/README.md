## Key Insights

### 1. Customer Support

Difficulty reaching effective customer support was one of the most recurring issues. Users reported problems with chatbot-based support, complaint resolution, and escalation.

**Recommended improvement:** Provide a clear human-agent escalation path and complaint tracking.

### 2. Delivery

Delivery delays and inaccurate estimated delivery times appeared repeatedly.

**Recommended improvement:** Improve real-time delivery tracking and ETA accuracy.

### 3. Pricing

Several reviews raised concerns about food prices, delivery fees, packaging charges, and other additional costs.

**Recommended improvement:** Provide a clearer breakdown of total charges and improve price transparency.

### 4. Order Accuracy

Some users reported missing, incorrect, or incomplete items.

**Recommended improvement:** Introduce stronger order verification and a simpler missing-item reporting process.

## Product Recommendations

| Recommendation                      | Priority |
| ----------------------------------- | -------- |
| Human support escalation            | High     |
| Improved delivery tracking          | High     |
| Transparent fee breakdown           | High     |
| Order verification                  | High     |
| Better cancellation/refund tracking | High     |
| Food quality monitoring             | Medium   |

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that the strongest improvement opportunities are concentrated around customer support, delivery reliability, pricing transparency, and order accuracy. These areas can be prioritized to improve customer satisfaction and reduce recurring complaints.

> **Note:** Findings are based on a limited sample of 15 collected reviews and should not be interpreted as representative of all Zomato users.
