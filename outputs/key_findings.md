# Key Findings

## Most Frequent Themes

1. **Customer Support — 7 mentions**

   * Users reported difficulty reaching effective support and resolving complaints.
   * **Priority:** High

2. **Delivery — 5 mentions**

   * Delivery delays and inaccurate estimated delivery times were recurring concerns.
   * **Priority:** High

3. **Pricing — 5 mentions**

   * Reviews highlighted high food prices, delivery fees, packaging charges, and other additional costs.
   * **Priority:** High

4. **Order Accuracy / Missing Items — 3 mentions**

   * Some users reported incorrect, incomplete, or missing items.
   * **Priority:** High

5. **Cancellation / Refund — 3 mentions**

   * Reviews included concerns about cancellation charges and refund handling.
   * **Priority:** High

## Recommended Product Improvements

| Priority | Recommendation                                        |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| High     | Add a clear human-agent escalation path               |
| High     | Improve real-time delivery tracking and ETA accuracy  |
| High     | Provide transparent pricing and fee breakdowns        |
| High     | Improve order verification and missing-item reporting |
| High     | Make cancellation and refund tracking clearer         |
| Medium   | Improve food-quality monitoring                       |
| Medium   | Provide more complete menu and quantity information   |

## Conclusion

The collected sample suggests that customer support, delivery reliability, and pricing transparency are the strongest areas for improvement. These findings were translated into prioritized product recommendations.

**Dataset size:** 15 reviews

**Note:** This is a small portfolio dataset and is not representative of all Zomato users.
